============================================================
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
============================================================

RoadGuard is a road-safety platform designed to help citizens identify, analyze, report, visualize, and track dangerous road conditions.

Users should be able to:

1. Upload a road image.
2. Analyze a road hazard.
3. Identify the type of hazard.
4. Estimate damage/severity.
5. Calculate a risk score.
6. Classify the hazard as LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH risk.
7. Save the hazard.
8. View hazards on an interactive map.
9. Submit complaints.
10. Track complaint status.
11. Generate/view reports.
12. View road-safety analytics.
13. View historical records.
14. Identify responsible authorities.
15. Manage road hazard information.

The application must have real navigation, real data flow, persistent storage, validation, loading states, error handling, and responsive design.
